# Olmera Healthcare - Visual Media Credits & Licensing

All imagery used across Olmera Healthcare is sourced from properly licensed photography repositories (Unsplash License, Creative Commons, and US Government Public Domain).

No AI-generated synthetic images and no stock clichés (handshakes, fake-smiling call-center headsets) are used. Every photograph represents authentic clinical environments, accredited hospital infrastructure, medical equipment, biological body systems, and qualified practitioners.

Consistent Color Grading: Images are lightly graded with an authentic clinical teal-tinted curve to unify ambient light and match Olmera's Deep Fern (#0E4F4A) aesthetic.
