# Olmera Patient Mobile User Guide

Welcome to **Olmera** (*"Care, quietly connected."*). This guide walks you through every patient feature available on Android and iOS.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Security
- **Sign In**: Open the Olmera app, choose **Patient**, and sign in with your registered email/OTP or demo credentials.
- **Privacy & Consent**: Under **Overview → Cross-Hospital Consent**, you control which accredited hospitals can view your diagnoses, lab reports, prescriptions, and scans—and can revoke access at any time.

## 2. Finding Doctors & Booking Appointments
1. Tap the **Book (+)** button on the bottom navigation bar (or **Book Consultation** in the menu).
2. Choose **Booking for Myself** or **Booking for Family Member** (add dependents via **Family Health**).
3. Select an accredited hospital, department, specialist, and real-time time slot.
4. Track your visit status, queue token, estimated wait time, and tap **I Have Arrived at Facility** in **Visits** when you reach the clinic.

## 3. Teleconsultation & End-to-End Encrypted Messaging
- Join video/audio consultations directly from **Visits** or **Telehealth**.
- Open **Messages** to chat securely with your doctor; all messages are protected with **AES-256-GCM** end-to-end encryption.

## 4. Health Records, Vitals & Wearables
- **Lab Reports**: View structured diagnostic results, AI-highlighted out-of-range biomarkers, and historical comparisons.
- **Vitals & Wearables**: Log Blood Pressure, Blood Glucose, Weight, Sleep, Steps, and Symptoms from **Overview**, or sync **Apple Health** / **Google Health Connect** via **Wearables Sync**.

## 5. Prescriptions, Reminders & Pharmacy Orders
- Open **Prescriptions** to view your daily medication schedule, mark doses taken or missed, and track adherence.
- Tap **Order Medicines** to choose **Home Delivery** (with courier tracking) or **Pharmacy Counter Pickup** (secured with a 6-digit pickup OTP).

## 6. Emergency SOS
- In an urgent situation, use **Emergency Assistance / SOS** to dial **108** immediately, share live location with consent, and view the nearest 24/7 trauma center.
