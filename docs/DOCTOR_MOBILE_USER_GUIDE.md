# Olmera Doctor Mobile User Guide

Welcome to the **Olmera Clinical Workstation** on Android and iOS. This guide covers daily clinical workflows for Junior Doctors, Senior Consultants, and Specialists.

---

## 1. Authentication & Role-Based Access
- Select **Specialist / Doctor** at sign-in.
- Your interface adapts to your clinical role (`junior_doctor`, `senior_consultant`, `specialist`) with HIPAA session controls and audit logging.

## 2. Daily Consultation Queue & Schedule
- **Home (`DoctorPortal`)**: View today's patient queue, upcoming visits, completed sessions, and quick metrics.
- **Schedule (`Visits`)**: Manage confirmed appointments, review patient arrival check-ins, and launch teleconsultations.

## 3. Patient Timeline, Pre-Visit Summary & Longitudinal Trends
- Tap **History** or open **Patients** to view:
  - AI pre-consultation summary
  - Unified patient timeline and organized lab reports
  - **Longitudinal Progress Chart** tracking multi-visit Blood Pressure, Fasting Glucose, and Weight trajectories
  - Medication adherence logs and cross-hospital records (when patient consent is active)

## 4. Encrypted Messaging & Prescriptions
- **Secure Chat**: Communicate with patients over **AES-256-GCM** end-to-end encrypted consultation threads.
- **E-Prescriptions**: Issue digital prescriptions that route directly to patient medication schedules and network pharmacies.

## 5. Clinical Hierarchy & Case Escalation
Inside **DoctorPortal → Clinical Hierarchy Console**:
- **Junior Doctors**: Document minor-concern call consultations, record vitals/provisional plans, and submit for senior supervisory review.
- **Senior Consultants**: Review pending junior doctor cases, add clinical guidance, approve/sign off decisions, or request revisions.
- **Specialists**: Receive escalated urgent/complex cases with preliminary diagnostics and record specialist recommendations.
